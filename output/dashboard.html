小试牛刀 · 每天、每周、每月、每年赚在哪、亏在哪Day, week, month and year: where it made and lost money

数字全部来自公开仓的账本，每个美股交易日收盘后自动更新。红色是赚，绿色是亏。点日历的任何一天、一周、一个月或一年，下面会列出那段时间每只股票贡献了多少。

Every number comes from the public ledger and updates after each US close. Red is a gain, green is a loss. Click any day, week, month or year and the list below shows how much each holding contributed.

NOTE

记录从 2026-11-12 收盘开始，基数 100。第一个完整交易日收盘后，这里才有第一笔 P&L。之前的日子不回填。

The record starts at the 2026-11-12 close, base 100. The first P&L appears after the first full trading day. Nothing before that is backfilled.

The level starts at 100. Comparisons: SPY, QQQ, and the holdings of the day before launch left untouched (Pre-launch).

Part II

P&L：日 · 周 · 月 · 年P&L by day, week, month and year

百分比是组合当期收益，括号里是指数点数（基数 100）。点格子或柱子，第三部分会换成那一段的收益来源。

Percent is the portfolio return for the period; the figure in brackets is index points (base 100). Click a cell or a bar to see that period's sources in Part III.

Part III

收益来源：哪些票赚了，哪些票亏了Sources: which holdings made money and which lost it

贡献 = 这只票当期每天的盈亏点数之和 ÷ 期初净值。所有行加起来等于组合当期收益，交易成本单列一行。

Contribution = the holding's daily P&L points summed over the period, divided by the level at the start. All rows add up to the portfolio return; trading cost is its own row.

Part IV

回撤来源：从高点跌下来时，是谁拖的Drawdowns: who pulled it down from the peak

回撤 = 净值相对此前最高点跌了多少。下面拆的是从高点到低点这段时间里，每只票各拖了多少。

Drawdown = how far the level sits below its previous high. The list splits the fall from peak to trough by holding.

Part V

口径与出处Method and sources

  • 价格：Yahoo 调整收盘（含分红）。账本文件：data/ledger/nav.csv、pnl_by_holding.csv、trades.csv。
  • 每天的盈亏 = 前一天收盘后持有的份额 × (当天收盘 − 前一天收盘)。调仓在当天收盘成交，从下一天起算新份额。
  • 交易成本按每笔成交金额 10 个基点扣，记在成本那一行。
  • 周按周一开始，月和年按自然月、自然年。期间收益是期初到期末的净值变化，不是每天收益相加。
  • 公司名和官网来自 Yahoo，ETF 的官网填发行商网站；中文名是常用叫法。logo 是官网图标（Google favicon 服务），缓存在 assets/logos/，取不到的画首字母。
  • 这是模拟组合的研究记录，不构成投资建议。
  • Prices: Yahoo adjusted closes (dividends included). Ledger files: data/ledger/nav.csv, pnl_by_holding.csv, trades.csv.
  • Daily P&L = units held after the previous close × (today's close − previous close). Trades fill at the day's close and count from the next day.
  • Trading cost is 10 bp of every traded dollar, shown as its own row.
  • Weeks start on Monday; months and years are calendar periods. A period's return is the change in level from start to end, not a sum of daily returns.
  • Company names and websites come from Yahoo; for ETFs the issuer's site is used. Logos are site icons from Google's favicon service, cached in assets/logos/; a letter badge stands in where none exists.
  • A simulated portfolio for research. Not investment advice.
